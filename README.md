# Emberlight_2D_Play_Offline
Ember saga original game/story. The 2D retro version
*Emberlight 2D — Peaksworn Rising** is a bite-size 2D action adventure that plays right in your browser — or downloads as a single file you can double-click and play offline forever.
Darkness called *the Shades* creeps over the mountain, and villages are being turned cold. You carry the Emberlight — a living flame saber that burns the dark back into light. From your home base, THE ASSEMBLY, you take missions from the board, earn tokens, level up, dress up... and when wind-up terrors march on the toyworks, you scoop snow and sticks and raise the defense with your own two hands.
## Features - THE ASSEMBLY hub — mission board, Glam Bazaar, token bank, jukebox - 25 missions — story beats, dailies and side quests across Easy / Medium /
Hard / Extreme (and a peak boss waits on Extreme) - Level up to 200 — five ember skills, ember ranks, the Toss Blade at level 10 - The Toy Siege — an overhead wave-defense arena: gather snow and sticks
from respawn piles, raise snowman guards and catapults anywhere you stand,
hold all 9 waves - Gentle beats between the fights — plant the terrace, cook the stew, walk
the lanterns, feed the barnyard, raise the barn, farm corn & beans, light six
watch camps on a long mountain patrol, carry a hurt fawn home to the healing
tent, and pull half-shaded villagers back into the light - The Glam Bazaar — outfits, hats, capes, pets, auras and blade styles,
including a holiday shelf: Merry Day, Hollow Crone, Heart Day, spring
parade, and the Fourth of July - Keeps your progress — autosaves in your browser; export/import save codes -
